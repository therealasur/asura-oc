How it works
🔄 SMART = inverted bias. Default — correct when OI builds strategically.
📊 RAW = writers are being forced out (margin calls / stop-outs). In this state, follow the Overall Bias hero card directly — the inversion is already correct for this scenario.
Triggers (Dynamic %):
📉 Bear crash: PE OI ↓>3.5% in 1 bar + spot fell 0.125% (≈30pts Nifty/95pts Sensex)
📈 Bull breakout: CE OI ↓>3.5% in 1 bar + spot rose 0.125%
🚪 Exit: Spot pulls back 0.25% (≈60pts Nifty) from trailing low/high — not from the original trigger. This means the exit tightens as price moves in your favour.
v5.0: OHLC Exit (fixed) — uses /v2/charts/intraday (1-min candles) for real per-bar HIGH/LOW, NOT the day-level OHLC. The last completed candle's LOW/HIGH is checked against the trailing exit level. Example: FU trailing high 24088, exit=24028. 14:06 candle LOW=24026.50 < 24028 → RAW exits to SMART immediately, even if snapshot spot recovered to 24040 by next fetch. ⚡OHLC shown in tooltip when this fires.
